# ggsegThalamus: Thalamic Nuclei Atlases for the 'ggsegverse' Ecosystem

Thalamic nuclei parcellations for the 'ggseg' ecosystem: the
diffusion-based grouping of Najdenovska et al. (2018) and the
structural-MRI THOMAS segmentation of Su et al. (2019). Provides
'ggseg_atlas' objects with 2D polygon geometry and 3D mesh data, for use
with 'ggseg' and 'ggseg3d'.
