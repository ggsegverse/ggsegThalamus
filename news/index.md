# Changelog

## ggsegThalamus 0.0.0.9000

- Added
  [`thalamus_hcp()`](https://ggsegverse.github.io/ggsegThalamus/reference/thalamus.md),
  the diffusion-based thalamic parcellation of Najdenovska et
  al. (2018): 7 nuclei groups per hemisphere.
- Added
  [`thalamus_thomas()`](https://ggsegverse.github.io/ggsegThalamus/reference/thalamus.md),
  the THOMAS structural-MRI segmentation of Su et al. (2019): 12 nuclei
  per hemisphere, separating the geniculate nuclei, habenula and
  mammillothalamic tract.
- Both are drawn in two coronal and two axial views on the cortical
  ribbon, with the other deep grey structures kept as context, and carry
  3D meshes.
