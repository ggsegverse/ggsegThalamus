# ggsegThalamus 0.0.0.9000

* Added `thalamus_hcp()`, the diffusion-based thalamic parcellation of
  Najdenovska et al. (2018): 7 nuclei groups per hemisphere.
* Added `thalamus_thomas()`, the THOMAS structural-MRI segmentation of
  Su et al. (2019): 12 nuclei per hemisphere, separating the geniculate
  nuclei, habenula and mammillothalamic tract.
* Both are drawn in two coronal and two axial views on the cortical ribbon,
  with the other deep grey structures kept as context, and carry 3D meshes.
